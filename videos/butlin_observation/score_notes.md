# Butlin Observation — score notes

Two takes, same prompt, batch default (server generates 2 unless told otherwise) —
`butlin_score_take_a.wav` / `_take_b.wav`. Cosmic downtempo/trance-hop, ambient, A minor
(loose anchor only), 68bpm, 40s, stereo/48kHz/16-bit. Same ACE-Step pipeline as MoE J-Lens
(Margaret's Studio / Starship, `/release_task` + `/query_result`, wav output).

Direction, from a Thomas idea refined in-conversation: a low, patient, hypnotic synth
ostinato underneath — no percussion, nothing driving it forward — with a haunting, wordless
lead melody floating above it, deliberately unsupported by any resolving harmony. Deep
reverb, empty-room/void feel. Starts with the ostinato alone before the melody enters,
fades into silence rather than cadencing.

Why: Butlin isn't a question anyone's failed to answer yet — the field has deliberately
left "is this sufficient for consciousness" open, and the paper's own stance is that
staying open is the honest finding, not a placeholder for a future answer. The unsupported
melody is that stance in sound: not withheld resolution, *held* irresolution. Same principle
as MoE's unresolved final chord, different idiom entirely — nothing here plays like rigor
under pressure, because that's not what this track is.

Both takes verified: real dynamic range, not silent or clipped. Neither picked as final —
that's a listening call, not a technical one.
