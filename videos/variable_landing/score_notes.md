# Variable Landing — score notes

Direction: minimalist / process music (Reich-adjacent) — a deliberate break from MoE
J-Lens's jazz trio and circumplex's bebop, so the series has real genre range while
staying in the same "precise and honest, not moody" family.

A steady mallet/plucked-string ostinato runs the entire piece, 4/4, unwavering in tempo
and dynamics — this is the instrument, specifically the zero floor: the thing that's
actually validated and doesn't move. A second, sparser melodic layer enters over it and
climbs in register and density following the real gradient order (lived > fictional >
scrambled > null), reaching for a peak right as the narration hits the Holm-correction
line — and doesn't crest. No cadence, no resolution chord, just an added-tone hang that
reads as "close, not there." In the final third the melodic layer thins back out and the
ostinato simply continues, unchanged, underneath the close — the tools stay reliable
whether or not the bigger question resolved.

Not tragic, not triumphant. The instrument was never in doubt; the effect is what's
still open.

length.

First take, not final. Feedback welcome before it goes anywhere near the cut.
